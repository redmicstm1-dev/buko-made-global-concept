# Asset and product-data notes

The logo and initial product SVGs were created for this project and contain embedded Buko Made branding. They are vector illustrations, not photos of real products and not endorsements by any manufacturer.

Before public launch, replace them with images you own or have permission to use. Keep a source/permission record for each image. Apply the watermark to the actual local image file, but do not cover product labels or safety information.

Product data is a curated initial catalogue, not a supplier-confirmed stock list. Confirm each model, specification, compatibility, regulatory documentation, price and availability before presenting it as a confirmed offer.
